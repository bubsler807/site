## Hot Air Balloon VR

You can land on Islands. You have a grappling hook that can pull the balloon in certain directions. Creatures will fly around you and try to climb into the basket. Different altitudes have different changing wind directions. Myst for the idiosyncratic, slightly steampunk aesthetic inspiration.

## Pixel Art Billboard MMO

Draw pixel art and put it into a 3d multiplayer world in the form of billboards.

## Parkour Game

No xwj but only hwj, meaning: can't hold a and w and spam d, but only press a and d to wall jump.

## Minimalist Ship Mud

Small server of 16 players or so. Can take on different rolls and work together to navigate the seas.

## NPC Creation MMO

MMO where you start playing on just one character, but eventually can turn that character into an npc that can give other players quests like fetch quests, or have them idling an activity. You can log back onto them and play them whenever. The world is player built in this way.

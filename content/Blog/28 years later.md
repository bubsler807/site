With the return of Danny Boyle and Alex Garland to the series comes the return of Christian imagery.

The film opens with a group of young children watching [Teletubbies](https://web.archive.org/web/20210218080819/https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teletubbies#Analysis).[^1] Soon enough all hell breaks loose. One of the children named Jimmy escapes the house as his mother is killed. He runs to nearby church, in which sits his father, a priest who interprets the outbreak as a sign of divine judgement. He gives Jimmy a crucifix necklace. He remains praying as the Zombies burst through the stained glass windows and he becomes infected in religious ecstasy.

28 years later, a small community lives on the island of Lindisfarne. We are introduced to Jamie, his wife Isla who has an unknown illness, and their 12 year old son, Spike.

Lindisfarne was a monastic island that functioned as center of spiritual and cultural synthesis in the 7th-8th centuries. In the film, Lindisfarne is cut off from the world. Vikings destroyed the historical Lindisfarne, and they return in symbolic form as the infected, specifically the Alpha berserker.

Jamie takes Spike across a causeway that floods with the tide, connecting the island to Great Britain. They come across some slow moving zombies and Spike kills one of them, while a couple others manage to sneak up behind them, but they are also killed, except for a child-zombie, which they spare.

They come to a building in which an infected has been hanged up by his feet and left for to be infected with a bag over his head. "JIMMY" has been carved into his flesh. Jamie tells his son to kill it, and that it will get easier the more he kills. He hesitates until it breaks loose and falls on the floor, then kills it.

They start heading back, then stumble across a deer head that has been mounted to a tree. Jamie is scared and says it was an Alpha. A rare type of zombie called an Alpha, with steroid-like mutation, no tremors, superior intelligence, and leadership over the other infected.

They run to a small building and hide in the attic.




Spike looks at the photo on the phone of his Fiance and she's a fish-lipped bimbo. He says that one of the members of their island community has the same thing when she eats shellfish, her face swells up. This highlights the absurdity of plastic surgery.

Unlike [[zombies|some zombie media]], 28 Years Later introduces a respect for the dead that the previous films did not possess with the introduction of the character Dr. Kelson.

An alpha is named Samson by Dr. Kelson. In the Old Testament, Samson is a Nazirite blessed with superhuman strength derived from his uncut hair. 

28 days later, Spike meets an adult Jimmy, wearing an inverted crucifix, a jumper, chains, totally blinged out and leading a cult-like gang.

> Got way too into Dracula last summer and kept writing songs about being in caves and hating the sun.[^1]

![[Pasted image 20250304115904.png]]

Discovered via an interview Dan Spencer had with Barrett Avner of the CONTAIN podcast. Country folk tinged with black metal.

The albums lyrics center around authenticity, humor, melancholy, death and desire from a chthonic perspective.

“It is a strange world, a sad world, a world full of miseries, and woes, and troubles. And yet when King Laugh come, he makes them all dance to the tune he play.” - A quote from Dracula that Spencer said he was "particularly struck by".[^2]

Favorite tracks: 
- Eternal Platitude
- Return to Your Dark Master

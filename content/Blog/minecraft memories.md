The first time I played Minecraft was at a friends house. I spawned in a desert and build a sand house and a small pyramid. At night a creeper came and blew my house up. I remember loving C418s music.

Back then, you could play an earlier version of the game in the browser, both single and multiplayer, that was a pure building game with no survival mechanics. I played that for a bit, but it didn't take long to get the full game some time in 2011.

The first server I joined was a small town, probably about 15 players. I built a small house in the form of a wood plank cube. My neighbor had built a giant pyramid out of gold. In the night, I got the idea to see if it was hollow on the inside, it wasn't, so I completely mined out the inside of it. About an hour later I got to witness him discovering this thievery. I never was discovered. I remember joining with someone who ran a shop selling wood logs for iron, which was a popular currency in the early days. I can't remember why I stopped playing on that server.

The first Minecraft youtuber I regularly watched was someone named PaperBatVG, a friend of the now famous videogamedunkey, who got his start on PaperBat's channel in a very funny series titled Beyond Good & Evil, where they played Dead Rising 2.

The first big PVP server I joined was the first big PVP server to ever exist called Chaos.Discovr.US. Many of the biggest minecraft youtubers of the time played on it such as PaulsoaresJr, Chimneyswift, Luclin, and some others, like the guys running the only Minecraft podcast around at the time called _The Shaft_, known for their famous Minecraft music video _In Search of Diamonds_. I think when I first joined it had a limit of 100 or 150 players. I eventually got banned for a reason I can't remember, but was unbanned a couple months later. I met someone named Dylan Wade, who's Youtube name was dylansmadness.

Back then the player base was so small that you could join a random server and the chances were pretty good that you would run into a friend or enemy from a previous server.

When the update adding wolves came out I got on a server. The spawn was a snow biome, which was perfect for finding wolves. I quickly punched some trees down, dug into a hill, and started mining down to find iron. Since I was so close to spawn, it wasn't long before someone came to the entrance and asked if they could join. I said sure. When wolves were first added, they were overpowered as fuck, so we spent a while killing anyone who spawned in by hitting them with a stick and watching them try to run away from our wolves.

There was a survival pvp server named mcpvp, which also had a quite popular hunger games mode. I occasionally played with the now infamous youtuber LeafyIsHere on that server, and was one of his first subscribers. He used to be quite funny, but this was years before he unfortunately started acting retarded for views.

## DarkRP

About a decade ago I used to play on servers called DarkRP servers. They were casual roleplaying servers. You can choose a job such as:
- Mayer
- Citizen
- Gun Dealer
- Police Officer
- S.W.A.T
- Mob Boss
- Gangster
- Thief
- Hitman
- Hacker
- Hobo
- Hobo King
- Medic
- Security Guard

Most players made money by hiding illegal money printers in their base. These printers could be heard through walls making suspicious humming noises and occasionally catching on fire.

In order to raid your base, police need a warrant, which means some guy has to advert "I heard noises", and hope that the mayor isn't afk.

Many Garry's Mod maps have a strange "liminal space" quality to them, mostly thanks to the square geometry and flat lighting.

One map, rp_bangclaw, was one of my favorites.

![[rp_bangclaw.jpg]]

Even above ground is quite creepy and feels like you're in some sort of surveillance box. Far better than most of the "liminal space" backrooms stuff of today. 

I used to use wiremod to scare people by puppeting ragdolls and throwing them at unsuspecting victims in the underground tunnels where people went to hide money printers or other dubious activities.

You can watch someone exploring it in Virtual Reality (which really highlights some of the strangeness of it) here: ![Exploring rp_Bangclaw](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iFF-63upgvM)

## Extended Movement Mod (Art of Parkour)

This has to be one of the most fun movement systems ever created. You can do all sorts of wall jumps, and exploit your momentum in various ways. You can learn the map (the classic gm_bigcity) and fly through it backwards. You can really master and abuse the physics engine. There are minigames such as tag, hunted (all the players are trying to catch the hunted, and when they catch them, they themselves become hunted), Deathmatch, and freeze-tag. 

I think I first got on the server in 2012. If I can remember correctly, the server was a simplified copy of some other servers. Not many people go on anymore. I've played hundreds of hours of it, but mostly stopped around 2018.

An explosive, kaleidoscopic rollercoaster of jangly pop music in the vein of Arial Pink, R. Stevie Moore, etc. 

One of the most interesting things about this album is how good he is at preserving and exemplifying the genres he drifts in and out of, even as they are occasionally superimposed on top of each other. It can be called superficial in this way.

## The Postmodern
The reader that takes Pynchon to be a postmodernist. For them, his novels are not a puzzle to be solved, but a game of deferring meaning. They believe the fact that Pynchon sabotages narrative closure to support this. They refer to how characters morph into symbols, and back again. How the plot unravels into entropy.
## The Vibe
"I don't know what's going on, but I like the way it feels!" The vibe reader floats through the novel like a stoner. They are not here for the plot, and they are not faking understanding. They are here for abstract texture, rhythm, slapstick, poetic flourishes. "I don't know why this line hit me so hard, but WOW!". They are right in a way, but let's be honest, this reader is usually kind of an idiot, just unknowingly skating across one of the densest bodies of work in American literature. They bring no tools, and don't leave with much at all.
## The Conspiracist
For this reader, Pynchon is not about paranoia, but rather about revealing conspiracies. The wacky names? Anagrams. The novels are encrypted messages about CIA operations, deep-state tech, postwar Nazi's... They are an act of whistleblowing.

## The Encyclopedist

It is sad that the above readers do not seem to come into contact with one another often, and if they do, they just dismiss the others. The reality is, Pynchon is as interested in 18th-century surveying techniques as he is about JFK or The Looney Tunes. Pynchon engages deeply in both Mythology and Parapolitics, and how these subjects interconnect. The world is a forest of mirrors.

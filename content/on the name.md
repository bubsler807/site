It is a modification on the title of the *Hypnerotomachia Poliphili*, an enigmatic, relatively comedic Renaissance text known for its elaborate, serpentine, proto-joycean prose in multiple languages, but mainly Italian, dreamlike structure and esoteric symbolism. The title itself translates roughly to *The Strife of Love in a Dream*.

# Strategic Confusion in the Wake of the Mechanization of the Desiring-Dream
--------

Hello. I'm an aspiring archivist, encyclopedist, and recordist. Yes, I have found myself in the age old battle with the surreally contingent nature of information preservation. Perhaps more importantly, there is also a contemplative, self-reflective, self-reflexive aspect to archival, and more specifically the cataloging, categorizing process... But that is a discussion for another page. The purpose of this site is principally to store and share both my own and others creations and observations. I will also document the various mistakes I will surely make along the way.
